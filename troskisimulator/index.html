Level 1 · The ride

Life on a tro tro

You drive. Your mate hangs out of the window shouting the destination. The passengers want to get there fast, safe and cheap. Troski Simulator puts you in the driver's seat of the vehicle that moves Ghana.

Passengers, fares & tips

Pick people up at tro tro stops, drop them where they are going, and collect the fare. Smooth driving keeps the comfort rating, and the tips, high. Your mate announces the stop from the pavement.

Police checkpoints

Officers wave you in, the barrier lifts only when you are cleared, and the cars queue behind you like real traffic. Nobody jumps the line.

Fuel, damage & the workshop

Fill up at stations on the roads, mind the potholes and the bumps, and repair at the mechanic when the van starts to complain. How hard you hit something decides how much it hurts.

Weather & night

Rain, storms, dust and floods that are never clean. The sun sets, the streetlights come on, and the markets light up with fireworks.

An open world

Main roads, side streets and village tracks across the city. Jams can start anywhere, so the shortcuts you learn are the ones that pay.

Make it yours

Paint, rims, stripes, number plates and the slogan on your windscreen. GOD'S TIME IS BEST never goes out of style.

Level 2 · Choose your shift

Four ways to work the road

Every shift starts at a random tro tro station or village somewhere in the city. Pick a mode, then pick your battles.
